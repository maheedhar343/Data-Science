03 / What You Will Master

Skills that span three disciplines

Computer Science

Python & Programming

NumPy, Pandas, OOP, APIs, file I/O, web scraping. The coding backbone of every data pipeline.

Mathematics

Statistics & Probability

Distributions, hypothesis testing, correlation, Bayesian reasoning — the language of data.

Machine Learning

Supervised & Unsupervised

Regression, classification, clustering, gradient boosting, neural networks, transfer learning.

Visualization

Data Storytelling

Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly dashboards — turning numbers into narratives that drive decisions.

Business

Problem Framing

Translating vague business questions into precise data problems. The most underrated DS skill.

Deployment

MLOps & Production

Flask APIs, Docker, CI/CD pipelines, model serialisation. From notebook to the real world.
